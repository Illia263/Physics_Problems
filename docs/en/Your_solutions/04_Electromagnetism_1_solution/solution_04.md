Here is the step-by-step breakdown for the fourth problem. This one is a classic physics exercise that perfectly illustrates why we completely ignore gravity when dealing with atoms!

### 4. Force Comparison

**Given:**
* Average distance: $r = 5.3 \times 10^{-11} \text{ m}$
* Charge of an electron/proton: $e = 1.60 \times 10^{-19} \text{ C}$
* Mass of an electron: $m_e = 9.11 \times 10^{-31} \text{ kg}$
* Mass of a proton: $m_p = 1.67 \times 10^{-27} \text{ kg}$
* Coulomb's constant: $k = 8.99 \times 10^9 \text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{C}^2$
* Gravitational constant: $G = 6.67 \times 10^{-11} \text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{kg}^2$

**Goal:** Calculate $F_e$, $F_g$, and the ratio $F_e/F_g$.

---

#### Step 1: Calculate the Electric Force ($F_e$)
We use Coulomb's Law, just like in the first problem. Because we only care about the *magnitude* of the force, we drop the negative sign on the electron's charge.

$$F_e = k \frac{|q_e q_p|}{r^2}$$

Plug in our constants and the distance:
$$F_e = \frac{(8.99 \times 10^9)(1.60 \times 10^{-19})^2}{(5.30 \times 10^{-11})^2}$$
$$F_e = \frac{(8.99 \times 10^9)(2.56 \times 10^{-38})}{28.09 \times 10^{-22}}$$
$$F_e = \frac{23.01 \times 10^{-29}}{28.09 \times 10^{-22}}$$
$$F_e = 8.19 \times 10^{-8} \text{ N}$$

#### Step 2: Calculate the Gravitational Force ($F_g$)
Now we switch to Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation. Notice how similar the structure of this formula is to Coulomb's Law—it's just masses instead of charges, and $G$ instead of $k$.

$$F_g = G \frac{m_e m_p}{r^2}$$

Plug in the masses and the gravitational constant:
$$F_g = \frac{(6.67 \times 10^{-11})(9.11 \times 10^{-31})(1.67 \times 10^{-27})}{(5.3 \times 10^{-11})^2}$$
$$F_g = \frac{101.47 \times 10^{-69}}{28.09 \times 10^{-22}}$$
$$F_g = 3.61 \times 10^{-47} \text{ N}$$

#### Step 3: Compare the two forces
To find out exactly how much stronger electricity is than gravity inside an atom, we divide the electric force by the gravitational force.

$$\text{Ratio} = \frac{F_e}{F_g}$$
$$\text{Ratio} = \frac{8.19 \times 10^{-8}}{3.61 \times 10^{-47}}$$
$$\text{Ratio} \approx 2.27 \times 10^{39}$$

---

**Final Answer:**
* **Electric Force:** $8.19 \times 10^{-8} \text{ N}$
* **Gravitational Force:** $3.61 \times 10^{-47} \text{ N}$
* **Ratio:** The electric force is roughly **$2.27 \times 10^{39}$ times stronger** than gravity! That is a 2 followed by 39 zeros. This is why gravity is totally irrelevant when calculating the interactions between subatomic particles.
