**Given Parameters:**

- Area ($S$) = **$0.02 \text{ m}^2$**
- Distance ($d$) = 5 mm = **$0.005 \text{ m}$**
- Voltage ($U_{\text{volts}}$) = **$500 \text{ V}$**
- Permittivity of free space ($\varepsilon_0$) $\approx$ **$8.854 \times 10^{-12} \text{ F/m}$**

---

**1. Capacitance ($C$):**
$$ C = \frac{\varepsilon_0 \cdot S}{d} $$
$$ C = \frac{(8.854 \times 10^{-12}) \cdot 0.02}{0.005} $$
$$ C = 3.54 \times 10^{-11} \text{ F} $$
**Answer:** The capacitance is **$35.4 \text{ pF}$\*\*.

---

**2. Energy Stored ($U_{\text{energy}}$):**
$$ U*{\text{energy}} = \frac{1}{2} C \cdot U*{\text{volts}}^2 $$
$$ U*{\text{energy}} = \frac{1}{2} (3.54 \times 10^{-11}) \cdot (500)^2 $$
$$ U*{\text{energy}} = 4.43 \times 10^{-6} \text{ J} $$
**Answer:** The energy stored is **$4.43 \text{ \mu J}$\*\*.

---

**3. Electric Field Intensity ($E$):**
$$ E = \frac{U\_{\text{volts}}}{d} $$
$$ E = \frac{500}{0.005} $$
$$ E = 100,000 \text{ V/m} $$
**Answer:** The electric field intensity is **$1.0 \times 10^5 \text{ V/m}$\*\*.

---

**4. Force of Attraction ($F$):**
$$ F = \frac{1}{2} \varepsilon_0 \cdot E^2 \cdot S $$
$$ F = \frac{1}{2} (8.854 \times 10^{-12}) \cdot (100,000)^2 \cdot 0.02 $$
$$ F = 8.85 \times 10^{-4} \text{ N} $$
**Answer:** The force of attraction is **$8.85 \times 10^{-4} \text{ N}$\*\*.
