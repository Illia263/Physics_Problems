---

**Given Parameters:**

* Area ($S$) = **0.02 m²**
* Distance ($d$) = **5 mm** = **0.005 m**
* Voltage ($U_{\text{volts}}$) = **500 V**
* Permittivity of free space ($\varepsilon_0$) $\approx$ **$8.854 \times 10^{-12}$ F/m**

### 1. Capacitance ($C$)

The formula for the capacitance of a parallel-plate capacitor is:

$$C = \frac{\varepsilon_0 \cdot S}{d}$$

$$C = \frac{(8.854 \times 10^{-12}) \cdot 0.02}{0.005}$$

$$C = 3.54 \times 10^{-11} \text{ F}$$

**Answer:** The capacitance is **35.4 pF**.

### 2. Energy Stored ($U_{\text{energy}}$)

The formula for the energy stored in a capacitor is:

$$U_{\text{energy}} = \frac{1}{2} C \cdot U_{\text{volts}}^2$$

$$U_{\text{energy}} = \frac{1}{2} (3.54 \times 10^{-11}) \cdot (500)^2$$

$$U_{\text{energy}} = 4.43 \times 10^{-6} \text{ J}$$

**Answer:** The energy stored is **4.43 µJ**.

### 3. Electric Field Intensity ($E$)

The formula for the uniform electric field between the plates is:

$$E = \frac{U_{\text{volts}}}{d}$$

$$E = \frac{500}{0.005}$$

$$E = 100,000 \text{ V/m}$$

**Answer:** The electric field intensity is **100,000 V/m** (or **$1.0 \times 10^5$ V/m**).

### 4. Force of Attraction ($F$)

The formula for the force between the plates is:

$$F = \frac{1}{2} \varepsilon_0 \cdot E^2 \cdot S$$

$$F = \frac{1}{2} (8.854 \times 10^{-12}) \cdot (100,000)^2 \cdot 0.02$$

$$F = 8.85 \times 10^{-4} \text{ N}$$

**Answer:** The force of attraction is **$8.85 \times 10^{-4}$ N**.
