**Concept:**
The color of light is determined by its wavelength within the visible spectrum (roughly 400 nm to 700 nm). The relationship between wavelength ($\lambda$), frequency ($f$), and the speed of light ($c$) in a vacuum is given by the wave equation $c = \lambda f$.

**Given:**

- Wavelength ($\lambda$) = 550 nm = **$550 \times 10^{-9} \text{ m}$**
- Speed of light ($c$) $\approx$ **$3 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s}$**

---

**1. Color in the Visible Spectrum:**
A wavelength of 550 nm falls squarely in the middle of the human visible spectrum. This specific wavelength corresponds to the **green** or **yellow-green** region, which is the peak sensitivity range for human photopic (daylight) vision.

**2. Frequency of the Light:**
**Formula:**
$$ f = \frac{c}{\lambda} $$

**Calculation:**
$$ f = \frac{3 \times 10^8}{550 \times 10^{-9}} $$
$$ f = \frac{3}{5.5 \times 10^{-7}} $$
$$ f \approx 0.545 \times 10^{15} \text{ Hz} $$
$$ f \approx 5.45 \times 10^{14} \text{ Hz} $$

**Answers:**

- The color corresponds to **yellow-green** (or green).
- The frequency of this light is approximately **$5.45 \times 10^{14} \text{ Hz}$** (or $545 \text{ THz}$).
