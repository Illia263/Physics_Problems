**Concept:**
In the electromagnetic spectrum, wavelength is inversely proportional to frequency and energy. To list the types of radiation in order of increasing wavelength, we must start with the highest energy (shortest wavelength) and end with the lowest energy (longest wavelength).

**Given List:**
Infrared, Ultraviolet, Microwaves, X-rays, Radio waves, Gamma rays.

**Order of Increasing Wavelength (Shortest to Longest):**

1.  **Gamma rays** (Shortest wavelength, highest energy)
2.  **X-rays**
3.  **Ultraviolet**
4.  **Infrared**
5.  **Microwaves**
6.  **Radio waves** (Longest wavelength, lowest energy)

**Answer:**
Gamma rays, X-rays, Ultraviolet, Infrared, Microwaves, Radio waves.
