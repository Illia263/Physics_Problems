**Concept:**
According to Gauss's Law, the total electric flux ($\Phi_E$) through a closed surface is equal to the net charge enclosed ($q_{\text{enc}}$) divided by the permittivity of free space ($\varepsilon_0$). The radius of the sphere does not affect the total flux.

**Given:**

- Enclosed charge ($q_{\text{enc}}$) = **2 C**
- Permittivity of free space ($\varepsilon_0$) $\approx$ **$8.854 \times 10^{-12}$ F/m**

**Formula:**
$$ \Phi*E = \frac{q*{\text{enc}}}{\varepsilon_0} $$

**Calculation:**
$$ \Phi_E = \frac{2}{8.854 \times 10^{-12}} $$

$$ \Phi_E \approx 2.259 \times 10^{11} \text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{C} $$

**Answer:**
The electric flux through the spherical surface is approximately **$2.26 \times 10^{11} \text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{C}$**.
