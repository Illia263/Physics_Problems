**Concept:**
The speed of light in a specific medium ($v$) is calculated by dividing the speed of light in a vacuum ($c$) by the refractive index ($n$) of that medium.

**Given:**

- Index of refraction of diamond ($n$) = **2.42**
- Speed of light in vacuum ($c$) $\approx$ **$3.00 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s}$**

**Formula:**
$$ v = \frac{c}{n} $$

**Calculation:**
$$ v = \frac{3.00 \times 10^8}{2.42} $$
$$ v \approx 1.23966 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s} $$

**Answer:**
The speed of light in a diamond is approximately **$1.24 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s}$**.
